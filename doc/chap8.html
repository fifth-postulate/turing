8 Busy Beaver

It is well know that some problems are uncomputable. One such example is the Busy Beaver function.

The Busy Beaver function B(n) is the most number of steps, or shifts, a turing machine with n states on two symbols can take and still halt.

8.1 Example

In this example we show how to take a standard representation of such machines, construct a turing machine from it and let it run its course.

gap> LoadPackage("turing");;
gap> machine := TmBB("1RB1LB_1LA0LC_1RZ1LD_1RD0RA");
<machine q1 <tape empty>>
gap> TmRun(machine);
gap> TmStepCount(machine);
107
